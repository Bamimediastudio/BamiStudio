My approach

A simple process for moving an idea from concept to a clear, customer-facing visual system.

01

Discover

Understand the idea, opportunity and product.

02

Define

Clarify the audience, positioning and purpose.

03

Build

Develop the product story and visual direction.

04

Systemize

Create a consistent visual system across touchpoints.

05

Launch

Present, refine and prepare the concept for launch.

Brand management

Make the brand feel like one brand.

Good brand management is more than making attractive graphics. It is keeping the product, message, visual identity and customer experience aligned.

01

Position

Clarify what the product stands for, who it serves and why it should matter.

02

Systemize

Create repeatable rules for logo use, typography, color, imagery and packaging.

03

Present

Translate product features into visuals, campaigns, social content and sales materials.

04

Consistency

Keep every customer touchpoint recognizable, from packaging to digital content.

05

Grow

Develop new variants and campaign ideas without losing the core brand identity.
